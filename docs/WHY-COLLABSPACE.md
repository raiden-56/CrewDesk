# CollabSpace AI Team: Why It Exists and Who It's For

## In one sentence

CollabSpace AI Team lets you add AI helpers to your team and give them real work, while people stay in charge of anything important.

---

## The problem it solves

AI tools today are good at research, writing, summaries and analysis. But using them at work is messy:

- **Everyone uses AI their own way.** One person pastes text into a chatbot, another uses a different tool. Nothing is shared and nobody knows what was done.
- **AI can make mistakes.** It might send the wrong message, publish something too early or change a document it shouldn't touch.
- **Costs are hidden.** Nobody knows how much is being spent on AI until the bill arrives.
- **There's no record.** If something goes wrong, there's no way to see who asked for what, or what the AI actually did.
- **AI can see too much.** It's easy to give an AI access to documents it shouldn't read.

Because of these risks, many teams either don't use AI for real work at all, or use it without any safety rules.

---

## What CollabSpace does about it

Think of it as **hiring AI team members and managing them like real staff.**

| Like a real team... | ...CollabSpace gives you |
| --- | --- |
| Each person has a job title | Each AI helper has a **role** (Researcher, Planner, Reviewer, Writer, Data Analyst and more) |
| People get clear instructions | You write **instructions** for each AI helper: what to do, how to behave, what to avoid |
| Staff only get the keys they need | You choose exactly **which tools** each AI helper may use |
| Staff only read files they're allowed to | You choose **which documents and websites** each AI helper may read |
| A manager signs off on big decisions | Risky actions **wait for a person to approve** them first |
| Projects have budgets | You set **spending limits**, and work stops if a limit is reached |
| Work gets tracked | Every action is **recorded** so you can always look back |

---

## How it works, step by step

1. **Create an AI helper.** Pick a role, give it a name, write its instructions, and choose its tools and documents.
2. **Give it a task.** For example: *"Compare three storage vendors and write a short report."*
3. **Watch it work.** You can see each step live: what it searched, which tools it used, how long it took.
4. **Approve when needed.** If it wants to do something risky (send a message, publish content, change a document), it stops and asks. You see exactly what it wants to do, then say **Approve**, **Reject** or **Ask for changes**.
5. **Get the result.** Reports and files are saved in one place for the whole team.

You can also link several AI helpers into a **workflow**. For example:

> Researcher finds the facts → Reviewer checks them → a person approves → Writer creates the final report

## Who it's for

### Teams and managers
Teams that want help with everyday work like research, planning, writing documents, reviewing work, analysing data or drafting replies, without losing control.

### Project managers
Turn a goal into a plan and task list, then have an AI helper keep things moving while you approve the key steps.

### Team leads and reviewers
Stay the final decision-maker. Nothing sensitive happens without your sign-off.

### Business owners and admins
See what AI is costing, set budgets, decide who can do what, and keep a full history for audits.

### Companies careful about safety and rules
Organisations that need to prove *who did what and when*, keep AI away from private data, and stop mistakes before they happen.

## Who does what (roles)

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including workspace settings |
| **Admin** | Manage people, budgets, tools and integrations |
| **Agent Manager** | Create and edit AI helpers, workflows and knowledge |
| **Task Operator** | Give tasks to AI helpers and run workflows |
| **Approver** | Approve or reject risky AI actions |
| **Viewer** | Look at everything, change nothing |

---

## Why it's needed: the short version

- **Get more done.** AI helpers take on repetitive research, writing and checking work.
- **Stay safe.** People approve anything risky. AI can never approve its own actions.
- **Control costs.** Budgets and usage reports show where the money goes.
- **Keep everything in one place.** Tasks, reports, files and history are shared with the whole team.
- **Always know what happened.** A full record of every action, by people and by AI.

> **AI does the work. People stay in control.**
